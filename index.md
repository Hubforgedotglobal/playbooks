# HubForge Playbooks

HubForge Playbooks is a free library of practical educational resources for turning data into decisions. Published by HubForge Global Impact Network Foundation.

## Which playbook should I read?

- Choose **Designing Dashboards with AI** to plan and improve a dashboard using the six-stage DESIGN framework. Includes exercises and a 27-page PDF.
- Choose **Claude Dashboard Playbook** for a hands-on guide to prompts, connecting data, exploring results, tracing evidence, and sharing dashboards with Claude. Includes examples and a 12-page PDF.

## Read and download

## Consulting in M&E with AI

Follow the seven-stage CONSULT method to choose, contract and deliver monitoring
and evaluation assignments. Includes 18 chapters, hands-on tools, a 60-page
playbook, an 81-page pack with 36 printable templates, and a 94-page toolkit.

- [Read the consulting playbook](https://playbooks.hubforgeglobal.com/consulting-in-me-with-ai/)

## Giants of Evidence

Trace 24 lives and 67 ideas through the history of monitoring, evaluation and learning. This free, 606-page international edition explores seven idea families, nine decades of change and the thinkers who built the evidence field.
