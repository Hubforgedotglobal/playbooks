# Claude Dashboard Playbook

A hands-on HubForge playbook about building evidence dashboards with Claude, with an emphasis on tracing numbers to their sources. The downloadable PDF contains 12 pages.

## What does it cover?

- Dashboard anatomy and writing the first prompt.
- Connecting data and exploring features.
- Copy-ready prompt recipes.
- Choosing a dashboard format for the reader and task.
- Troubleshooting results.
- Sharing, access, cost, and privacy considerations.
- A cheat sheet, launch checklist, and common questions.

## Who is it for?

People who turn data into decisions, including monitoring, evaluation and learning teams, researchers, programme and operations leads, analysts, founders, and public officials.
