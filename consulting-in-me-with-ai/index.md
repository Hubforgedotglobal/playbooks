# Consulting in Monitoring & Evaluation with AI Playbook

A free HubForge playbook for solo consultants, small firms and teams working on
monitoring and evaluation assignments. Follow the seven-stage CONSULT method
from choosing work and contracting it well to delivering evidence that gets used.

## What is included

- 18 chapters and practical exercises, from a first assignment to a stronger consulting practice.
- A seven-stage method, worked examples, proposal and delivery tools, and guidance on using AI responsibly.
- An engagement studio, evaluation-matrix export, calculators, Word template downloads, and a searchable library of ideas.
- A 60-page playbook, an 81-page template pack with 36 printable templates, and a 94-page consulting toolkit.
- A one-page cheat sheet extracted from the supplied playbook.

The practical tools run in the browser. No sign-in or paid AI service is required
to use the playbook. Its AI prompts are suggestions to use with a tool of your
choice, not an automatic analysis service. Work saved by the exercises stays on
the reader's device. Shared ratings and anonymous activity use the separate
Playbooks reader-feedback service; written feedback is private until moderated.
