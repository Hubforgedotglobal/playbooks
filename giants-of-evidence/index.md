# Giants of Evidence

The lives and ideas that built monitoring, evaluation and learning. This free, 606-page international edition follows 24 thinkers, seven families of ideas, and nine decades of debate.

## How to use the book

- Read the [web edition](https://playbooks.hubforgeglobal.com/giants-of-evidence/) and open the built-in interactive stories and reflection tools.
- [View or download the International Edition B5 PDF](https://playbooks.hubforgeglobal.com/giants-of-evidence/giants-of-evidence-international-edition-b5.pdf).
- Rating, optional feedback and share links appear on the [web edition](https://playbooks.hubforgeglobal.com/giants-of-evidence/).

## Main themes

- Seven families of ideas that shaped evidence and evaluation.
- Nine decades of practical and intellectual change.
- Twenty-four stories and 67 influential ideas.
- Plain-language definitions, discussion, and sources.
